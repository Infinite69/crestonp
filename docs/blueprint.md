# **App Name**: Creston Design Studio

## Core Features:

- Intuitive Navigation & Information Display: Seamless navigation across Home, Services, Projects, About, and Contact pages, alongside clear display of business stats (e.g., projects completed, happy clients) and process steps.
- Dynamic Project Portfolio Showcase: An interactive image gallery grid displaying past projects with hover effects and placeholder images, enabling users to explore Creston Projects' work.
- Detailed Service Presentations: Dedicated sections for Lighting Design, Interior Design, and Outdoor Design, each featuring engaging icons and concise descriptions of services offered.
- Client Testimonials & Feedback: A rotating display of client reviews in a clean card layout, building trust and showcasing client satisfaction.
- Direct Consultation Inquiry Form: A simple and secure contact form for prospective clients to submit inquiries and 'Get Consultation' requests, linking to a backend service for submission handling.
- Adaptive & Animated User Interface: Fully responsive design for optimal viewing across devices, with smooth scrolling, subtle animations, and hover effects to enhance the premium user experience.
- AI Design Mood Board Tool: An AI-powered tool that allows users to input design preferences and receive curated mood board suggestions, offering initial design inspiration.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Deep Emerald Green (#046307). A rich, elegant tone used for headlines, key interactive elements, and brand accents, reflecting luxury and professionalism.
- Background Color: Very light off-white (#F8FAF8). A highly desaturated, almost neutral color with a faint cool green undertone, providing a clean, spacious, and calming backdrop.
- Accent Color: Muted Chartreuse (#C6D8AD). A soft, desaturated green-yellow for subtle highlights, hover states, and complementary iconography, offering warmth and sophistication without overpowering the primary green.
- Headline and Body Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif). A modern, objective, and highly legible typeface, selected for its clean aesthetic that aligns with minimalism and ensures excellent readability across all text.
- Minimalist line-art icons. Employing a consistent set of clean, line-based icons for services and key features, contributing to the site's elegant and understated visual language.
- Ample Whitespace & Clean Grid. A focus on generous negative space around elements and precise, symmetrical grid-based layouts to achieve a spacious, balanced, and sophisticated feel, akin to high-end portfolio sites.
- Subtle Hover Effects and Scroll-Triggered Fade-Ins. Implement delicate hover animations on buttons and images, combined with gentle scroll-triggered fade-in effects for sections, to add a premium, interactive, and smooth browsing experience without being distracting.
